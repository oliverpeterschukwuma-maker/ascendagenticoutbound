# Uploads — Drop Files Here

Place any of the following in this folder and tell the assistant:

| File type | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| Operating permit / licence | Fills in your actual permit limits, monitoring frequencies, reporting deadlines |
| Process flow diagram (PFD) or P&ID | Site layout map, equipment list, valve and instrument tags |
| Equipment manuals | Section 6 updated with your specific equipment, controls, settings, maintenance intervals |
| SOPs from your employer | Replaces generic procedures with your site's actual procedures |
| Chemical SDS or delivery records | Section 5 updated with your specific chemicals, concentrations, suppliers |
| Lab results or logbook samples | Helps calibrate the "normal" ranges in Section 1 and 4 |
| Training notes or photos | Any photos of equipment, screenshots of SCADA, or handwritten notes |
| Incident reports | Adds site-specific examples to Section 8 (Troubleshooting) and Section 10 (Emergency Response) |
| Org chart / contact list | Fills in names and numbers in Sections 0, 9, 10, 11 |

After uploading, tell the assistant: "I've added [description of files] to the uploads folder."
The assistant will read them and update the relevant manual sections.
