## Topics where no good video was found

- **BC-specific rules** (EOCP (Environmental Operators Certification Program), Municipal Wastewater Regulation, WorkSafeBC Part 9/10): use the official websites listed in [SOURCES-TO-GET.md](../SOURCES-TO-GET.md). WorkSafeBC also publishes its own safety videos — search "WorkSafeBC confined space video" and "WorkSafeBC lockout video" on worksafebc.com.
- **Anaerobic digester operation (volatile acids/alkalinity)**: search YouTube for "digester upset troubleshooting" or "VA/ALK (Volatile Acids to Alkalinity ratio) titration".
- **Metric kg/day formula**: most videos teach the US "pounds formula". The metric version (kg/d = mg/L (milligrams per litre) × m³/d (cubic metres per day) ÷ 1,000) works exactly the same way — see [Math 17C](../math/17c-loading-dosage-concentration.md).
