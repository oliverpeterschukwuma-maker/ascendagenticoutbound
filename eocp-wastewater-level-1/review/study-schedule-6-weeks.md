**Assumes:** 1–2 hours per day, 6 study days per week + 1 light day. **Math every single day** (marked 🔢), because math improves with daily repetition, not cramming.

**Daily session pattern (≈ 90 min):**
```
 10 min  Flashcard review (old cards first, then new)
 45 min  Main topic (read the module or do practice)
 25 min  🔢 Math practice
 10 min  Update the progress tracker + mistake notebook
```
Short on time (60 min)? Cut the main topic to 25 min — **never skip the math or the flashcards**.

---

## Writing next year? Start with the long-runway plan

If your exam is **6–12 months away**, don't start the 6-week plan yet. Use the extra time so that the final 6 weeks are review, not first-time learning.

| Stage | When | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 0 — Set up** | Now | Do the [verification checklist](../00-exam-overview.md#5-verification-checklist--do-this-once-early-on-45-min) · download the EOCP/WPI files ([SOURCES-TO-GET.md](../SOURCES-TO-GET.md)) · **count your operating hours toward 1,800** · ask EOCP (Environmental Operators Certification Program) the questions on that page | 1–2 sessions |
| **Stage 1 — First slow pass** | Months 1–3 | Read Module 0, then one module per week (Modules 1–18) · 15 min of flashcards daily · Math 17A in month 1, 17B in month 2, 17C in month 3 · **connect each module to your real plant** (find the equipment, read the SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures)) | 3–4 sessions/week |
| **Stage 2 — Second pass + quizzes** | Months 4–5 | Re-read each module's MUST MEMORIZE + traps · do every module quiz (log scores) · Math 17D and 17E · start the mistake notebook | 4–5 sessions/week |
| **Stage 3 — Final sprint** | Last 6 weeks | **Follow the 6-week plan below** | 6 days/week |

**Learning on the job is the best study tool.** Ask senior operators to explain equipment during rounds, read your plant's O&M (Operations and Maintenance) manual, and watch the lab tests being done. You need 1,800 hours anyway — make them count.

## Readiness check before booking

Book (or keep) your exam date when **all** of these are true:
- [ ] All module quizzes ≥ 80%
- [ ] Math 17E ≥ 70% (62/88), and you can do detention time, kg/day, F/M, SVI and pump power without notes
- [ ] Mock Exam 1 or 2 ≥ **85%**, no content area under **75%**
- [ ] You've counted your **1,800 operating hours** and your EOCP application is in

If you score **below 70%** on Mock 2: don't panic. Add **two more weeks** repeating Weeks 4–5 for your weakest areas, then re-take whichever mock you did worst on (the questions will be shuffled in your memory by then — focus on *why* answers are right).

## Tips for when math feels hard

- **Same formula, many times.** Do the detention-time examples on 3 different days. Repetition beats talent.
- **Say the units out loud.** "Cubic metres divided by cubic metres per day gives days."
- **Write the formula first**, then fill in the numbers.
- **Estimate before calculating.** A clarifier DT should be a few hours; if you get 300, look for a units mistake.
- **Keep a "my mistakes" list** for math only (e.g., "I forget × 24", "I use 65 not 0.65"). Read it before every math session.
- **It's fine to go slowly.** At the exam you have time — about 1.5–1.8 minutes per question, and most questions aren't math.
