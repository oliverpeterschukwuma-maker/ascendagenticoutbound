## Answer key with explanations

| # | Ans | Area | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **B** | SA | The 4:1 rule; extend about 1 m above the landing and keep three points of contact. |
| 2 | **A** | EQ | Timing the drop in a calibration column shows the real feed rate (mL/min), which can be compared with the setting. |
