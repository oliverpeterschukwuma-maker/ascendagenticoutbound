This is **how** to study. The [schedule](../review/study-schedule-6-weeks.md) tells you **when**.

```
PHASE 1 LEARN ──► PHASE 2 PRACTICE ──► PHASE 3 FIND WEAKNESSES ──► PHASE 4 MIXED ──► PHASE 5 MOCK EXAMS
 (read modules)    (module quizzes)      (tracker + review)          (shuffled Qs)     (full timed exams)
        ▲                                         │
        └───────────── loop back for weak topics ─┘
```

---

## Phase 1 — Learn

For **every module**, in order (Module 0 first):

1. **Read the checklist** ("What you need to know") so you know the goal.
2. **Read the plain-English teaching** slowly. Say the key ideas **out loud** in your own words.
3. **Draw the diagrams yourself** on paper (process flows, pump parts, the solids family tree). Drawing = remembering.
4. **Read "Operator-level understanding"** and picture yourself doing it at a plant.
5. **Copy the "MUST MEMORIZE" list** by hand into a notebook. (Writing by hand helps memory.)
6. **Make or review the flashcards** for that module ([flashcards](../review/flashcards.md)).
7. **Read the "Common exam traps"** — these are where most marks are lost.

**Quiz yourself instead of re-reading** (EOCP (Environmental Operators Certification Program) *Operator Digest*): testing yourself — flashcards, practice questions, **fill-in-the-blank** notes like *"The radius is one-half the ______"* — sticks far better than reading the same page again.

**Memory method — spaced repetition:** review new flashcards **the same day**, then **1 day later**, **3 days later**, **1 week later**, **2 weeks later**. Cards you get wrong go back to "tomorrow".

---

## Phase 2 — Practice (topic-specific)

- Do each module's **quiz (section 7)** **without looking** at the notes.
- **Mark** answers, then read **every explanation** — including the ones you got right (you may have guessed).
- For math, do the **practice sets in 17A–17D**, then the **88-problem bank (17E)** in blocks of 10–15.
- **Log every quiz** in the [progress tracker](progress-tracker.csv).

**Target:** **80%+** on each module quiz before moving on. Below 80% → re-read the sections you missed and redo the quiz after 2 days.

---

## Phase 3 — Weakness identification

Use the **[progress tracker](progress-tracker.csv)** (opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers). For every quiz/practice set it records:

| Column | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Date | When you did it |
| Phase | 2, 4 or 5 |
| Source | e.g., "Module 6 quiz", "17E #1–15", "Mock 1" |
| Topic / Module | e.g., "Secondary clarification" |
| WPI (Water Professionals International) Content Area | Equipment / Process / Safety-Admin / Lab / Math |
| Attempted | number of questions |
| Correct | number right |
| Incorrect | calculates automatically |
| % | calculates automatically |
| Missed question numbers | e.g., "3, 7, 12" |
| Why I missed them | **didn't know / misread / math error / unit error / trap** |
| Next action | e.g., "re-read 6.2.8; redo quiz Thursday" |

**Every week, look for patterns:**
- **Any topic under 70%** → weak area. Go back to Phase 1 for that module.
- **Same mistake type repeating** (e.g., unit errors, forgetting × 24, "NOT/EXCEPT" misreads) → write it on a sticky note on your desk.
- **Content-area totals** — compare to the real exam weights: **Equipment 39% · Process 38% · Safety/Admin 13% · Lab 10%**. A weak *Equipment* score costs the most marks.

**The "mistake notebook":** for every missed question, write:
1. The question (short version)
2. The right answer
3. **Why** the right answer is right (one sentence)
4. **What fooled you**

Read the mistake notebook **every few days**. This is the most effective study tool in the whole system.

---

## Phase 4 — Mixed practice

Once you've finished all modules:
- Do the **[60-question mixed practice set](mixed-practice.md)** — topics are shuffled, so you have to **recognize** what kind of question it is (just like the real exam).
- Redo the **module quizzes in random order** (pick a module number out of a hat).
- Mix in **10 math problems** from 17E every session.
- Score by **content area** using the table at the bottom of each set.

---

## Phase 5 — Mock exams

- **[Mock Exam 1](mock-exam-1.md)** — 100 questions, weighted like the real WPI exam (39/38/13/10).
- **[Mock Exam 2](mock-exam-2.md)** — 100 **different** questions, same weights.
- **[EOCP official practice material](eocp-official-practice.md)** — the EOCP (Environmental Operators Certification Program) sample exams and math questions (download them yourself; links on that page).

**How to take a mock properly:**
1. **Same conditions as the real exam:** quiet room, timer (3 hours — confirm the real limit), calculator, formula sheet only.
2. **Strategy:** go through all 100 once; answer the easy ones; **flag** long math or unsure questions; come back to flagged ones; **never leave a blank**.
3. **Pace:** about **1.5–1.8 minutes per question**. At the halfway time you should be past question 50.
4. **Score by content area** (table at the bottom of each mock).
5. **Review every wrong answer** into your mistake notebook.
6. Wait **at least a week** and fix weak areas before the next mock.

**Readiness rule of thumb:** you're ready to book/write when you score **85%+ on a mock you haven't seen before**, with **no content area below 75%**. (The pass mark is 70% — the extra margin covers exam-day nerves and new question wording.)

## Exam-day checklist

- [ ] Confirmed time and location with EOCP; tried **"Experience ABC (Association of Boards of Certification)'s Computerized Exams"** demo beforehand
- [ ] **Government photo ID**: driver's licence, BC Services Card, or passport (checked before you get the exam)
- [ ] **Arrive early** — more than 30 minutes late = you can't write (and late arrivals lose time)
- [ ] Need to reschedule? Do it **more than 7 days ahead** to avoid a fee (EOCP: 1-866-552-3627, eocp@eocp.ca)
- [ ] At the exam you'll receive the **ABC Canadian Formula/Conversion Table (Wastewater)**, **EOCP Formulae, Conversions & Abbreviations**, **EOCP Conversion Chart** and **candidate instructions** — you've practised with copies of these
- [ ] **Non-programmable** calculator (fresh battery) — the same one you practised with. **Closed book**: no notes; scratch paper is provided
- [ ] Know the buttons ([Help window](../sources/ABC-candidate-instructions-summary.md#the-help-window--from-the-official-sample-test)): ☐ = mark · `>>` = next unanswered/marked · Time = time left
- [ ] Read the **Help** window first · `>>` = next unanswered · flag questions and come back · **blank = wrong**
- [ ] Need a break? Click **"Cover"** (never "Exit") → "Resume" when back — the clock keeps running
- [ ] Finish: **Cover** → check all answered → **Finish and display results** → **Mastery** (your score shows) → **Exit**
- [ ] Slept well; ate; **drank water before** — ✅ EOCP: **no food or drinks (not even water), no hats, no electronics** (phones, **smart watches**, tablets) in the exam room
- [ ] Pen/pencil for working out math (scratch paper is provided)
- [ ] Arrive early
- [ ] Read the **last line** of each question first; watch **NOT / EXCEPT / FIRST / MOST LIKELY**
- [ ] **Safety first → verify → check equipment → small change** for "what should the operator do" questions
- [ ] **π = 3.14**; units checked; answers sanity-checked
- [ ] Flag and return; answer everything
- [ ] **Read every word** — e.g., "**two** clarifiers" doubles the volume (EOCP's own example of a missed word)
- [ ] **Trust your first answer** — only change it if you find a clear mistake (EOCP trainers: people who fail by 2–3 questions often changed right answers to wrong ones)
- [ ] Review at the end for blanks or double answers
