**Source:** *EOCP (Environmental Operators Certification Program) Math Test Questions* (9 pages, 50 multiple-choice questions + answer sheet), from the EOCP *Preparing for Your Exam* page. Questions are written here in short form; **every answer below matches EOCP's own answer sheet** and was re-checked by calculation.

> **⚠ About π (pi) in this set:** EOCP's answer choices for circles were worked with π ≈ 3.1416 or 22/7, so with **π = 3.14** (or 0.785 × D²) your answer is **slightly off** — e.g., 339.1 vs the choice 339.4. **Pick the closest choice.** The choices are always far apart, so this never changes the right answer.

> **What this set teaches:** most EOCP math is **unit conversion, percent, area/volume, and "how long to fill"**. Watch for **distractors** — extra numbers you don't need (Q15, 21, 35, 37, 39) — and **rounding up** for whole items (Q1, 21).

**How to use:** do 10 at a time with a calculator and the formula sheet, cover the solutions, then check. Log your score in the [progress tracker](progress-tracker.csv).

## Answers with step-by-step solutions

| # | Answer | How to solve it | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **a** | 333 ÷ 50 = 6.66 pipes. You can't buy part of a pipe, and 6 pipes (300 m) is too short → **round UP to 7**. | Whole-unit rounding |
| 2 | **c** | 1 m³ = 1,000 L, so L → m³ means **÷ 1,000**: 18,500 ÷ 1,000 = **18.5 m³/h**. | Unit conversion |
| 3 | **a** | 1 minute = 60 s = 3 × 20 s → 475 × 3 = 1,425 mL/min. mL → L ÷ 1,000 → **1.425 L/min**. | Pump calibration |
| 4 | **c** | "How many more" = subtract: 15,305 − 3,927 = **11,378**. | Subtraction |
| 5 | **b** | 350 − 120 = **230 mg/L**. | Subtraction |
| 6 | **d** | m³ → L: × 1,000 → 530 L/s. Per second → per minute: × 60 → **31,800 L/min**. | Unit conversion |
| 7 | **a** | 15,500 − 3,503 − 4,509 = **7,488 L**. | Subtraction |
| 8 | **b** | 2 weeks = 14 days: 1,080,000 × 14 = **15,120,000 L**. | Multiplication |
| 9 | **b** | 24 h × 60 = 1,440 min. 275 × 1,440 = **396,000 mL**. | Time conversion |
| 10 | **c** | 53% = 0.53 → 0.53 × 1,270,000 = **$673,100**. | Percent |
| 11 | **a** | 36% blocked → 100 − 36 = **64% still flows**. 0.64 × 5,000 = **3,200 L/min**. (Trap: 0.36 × 5,000 = 1,800 is the *lost* flow.) | Percent |
| 12 | **c** | Part ÷ whole × 100 = 6 ÷ 18 × 100 = **33%**. | Percent |
| 13 | **b** | Accuracy = meter reading ÷ actual × 100 = 23,503 ÷ 31,337 × 100 = **75%** (the meter under-reads). | Percent |
| 14 | **a** | 100 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = **10%**. | Percent |
| 15 | **d** | Area = length × width = 75 × 37.5 = **2,812.5 m²**. The height is a **distractor** (it's only needed for volume). | Area (rectangle) |
| 16 | **a** | V = 0.785 × D² × H = 0.785 × 6 × 6 × 12 = **339.1 m³** → closest choice **339.4**. | Volume (cylinder) |
| 17 | **d** | C = π × D = 3.14 × 30 = **94.2 cm** → closest **94.28**. | Circumference |
| 18 | **c** | Cone = ⅓ × 0.785 × D² × H = ⅓ × 0.785 × 2,500 × 3 = **1,962.5 m³** → closest **1,964.28**. (A cone is exactly ⅓ of the cylinder with the same base and height.) | Volume (cone) |
| 19 | **a** | A = 0.785 × D² = 0.785 × 30 × 30 = **706.5 cm²** → closest **707.14**. | Area (circle) |
| 20 | **a** | Same as Q9 (396,000 mL), then mL → L ÷ 1,000 = **396 L**. | Time + unit conversion |
| 21 | **c** | 77.5 ÷ 1.5 = 51.67 → **round UP to 52** (51 sections would leave a gap). The 200 mm size is a distractor. | Whole-unit rounding |
| 22 | **c** | Each metre of water = **9.8 kPa** (ABC (Association of Boards of Certification) sheet). 10 × 9.8 = **98 kPa**. | Pressure |
| 23 | **b** | Used = 7,399.6 − 6,678.6 = 721 m³ over **7 days** → 103 m³/day × 1,000 = **103,000 L/day**. | Totalizer → average flow |
| 24 | **b** | Year 4 = end of Y4 − end of Y3 = 2,371.4 − 2,007.1 = **364.3 h**. | Meter readings |
| 25 | **c** | V = 6 × 12 × 9 = 648 m³ = 648,000 L. Time = 648,000 ÷ 15 = 43,200 s ÷ 3,600 = **12 h**. | Fill time |
| 26 | **d** | D = 20 cm = **0.2 m**. V = 0.785 × 0.2² × 600 = 18.84 m³ × 1,000 = **18,840 L** → **18,850**. | Volume (pipe) |
| 27 | **b** | 9 × 24 = **216 m²**. | Area (rectangle) |
| 28 | **b** | Total = 196.4 km ÷ **5 days** (Thursday's 0 still counts as a day) = **39.3 km**. | Average |
| 29 | **a** | 6 h × 3,600 = 21,600 s. 30,000 ÷ 21,600 = **1.39 L/s**. | Flow rate |
| 30 | **b** | DT = volume ÷ flow = 450 ÷ 0.5 = 900 s ÷ 60 = **15 min**. | Detention time |
| 31 | **d** | 12 × 3.5 × 1.5 = 63 m³ × 1,000 = **63,000 L**. | Volume (rectangle) |
| 32 | **c** | D = 300 mm = **0.3 m**. V = 0.785 × 0.09 × 600 = 42.39 m³ × 1,000 = **42,390 L** → **42,412**. | Volume (pipe) |
| 33 | **b** | Make units match first: 588 cm = **5.88 m**. 5.88 × 6.5 = **38.2 m²**. | Area + unit mixing |
| 34 | **d** | 2,699 − 1,857 = 842 L ÷ 7 = **120 L/day**. | Totalizer → average flow |
| 35 | **b** | Only the **drop** matters: 11.6 × 7.5 × 0.3 = 26.1 m³ = **26,100 L**. The 4.6 m height is a distractor. | Volume (level change) |
| 36 | **a** | % removal = (in − out) ÷ in × 100 = (900 − 663) ÷ 900 × 100 = 237 ÷ 900 × 100 = **26.3%**. | % removal |
| 37 | **a** | kg/day = mg/L × m³/d ÷ 1,000 = 2,900 × 4,200 ÷ 1,000 = **12,180 kg/d**. Diameter and depth are **distractors**. | kg/day formula |
| 38 | **a** | Floor area = 2.4 × 1.8 = 4.32 m². 850 L = 0.85 m³. Rise = 0.85 ÷ 4.32 = 0.197 m. New height = 1.2 + 0.2 = **1.4 m**. | Volume ↔ height |
| 39 | **b** | Top = length × width = 24 × 12 = **288 m²** (height is a distractor). | Area (rectangle) |
| 40 | **a** | Cylinder = 0.785 × 9² × 12 = 763.0 m³. Cone = ⅓ × 0.785 × 9² × 2.5 = 53.0 m³. Total = **816 m³** → **816.7**. | Cylinder + cone |
| 41 | **b** | 0.785 × 5² × 2.5 = 49.06 m³ × 1,000 = **49,060 L** → **49,087** (49.1 is the m³ answer — wrong units). | Volume (cylinder) |
| 42 | **b** | 3,105 ÷ 3 = 1,035 m³/d × 1,000 = **1,035,000 L/day**. | Flow rate |
| 43 | **a** | V = 0.785 × 25 × 3.9 = 76.5 m³ = 76,540 L. Time = 76,540 ÷ 2,700 = **28.3 h** → **28.4**. | Fill time |
| 44 | **d** | 4,800 ÷ 320 = **15 min**. | Fill time |
| 45 | **a** | DT = 2,500 ÷ 10 = **250 days**. | Detention time |
| 46 | **c** | Sphere V = π × D³ ÷ 6 = 3.14 × 45³ ÷ 6 = 47,689 cm³. 1,000 cm³ = 1 L → **47.7 L**. | Volume (sphere) |
| 47 | **c** | 0.785 × 0.75² × 1.5 = 0.6623 m³ × 1,000 = **662 L** → **662.9**. | Volume (cylinder) |
| 48 | **d** | Demand = dose − residual = 8.7 − 2 = **6.7 mg/L**. | Chlorine demand |
| 49 | **c** | mg/L × L = mg: 50 × 250,000 = 12,500,000 mg ÷ 1,000,000 = **12.5 kg**. (Or: 250,000 L = 250 m³ → 50 × 250 ÷ 1,000 = 12.5 kg.) | kg formula |
| 50 | **a** | Product needed = pure amount ÷ purity = 1.5 ÷ 0.75 = **2 kg**. (Check: 75% of 2 kg = 1.5 kg ✔.) You always need **more** product than pure chemical. | % strength / hypochlorite |
