## About this page

EOCP (Environmental Operators Certification Program) publishes some practice material on its website, but the workspace this course was built in **can't open eocp.ca** (network blocked). So the official questions couldn't be read or included. Everything else in this course is **original practice material written in the WPI (Water Professionals International) style** — not real exam questions.

**When you download the files below and give them to Claude** (attach them in chat, or upload them to the `sources/` folder on GitHub — see [SOURCES-TO-GET.md](../SOURCES-TO-GET.md)), Claude can:
- add the questions here **with worked answers and explanations**,

## How to use them (once you have them)

1. **Do the OIT sample exam in Week 1** — just to see the style. Don't worry about the score.
2. **Do the EOCP Math Test Questions** in Weeks 3–5, alongside Module 17.
3. **Do the SWWS sample exam in Week 5**, timed, as extra mixed practice.
4. Log scores in the [progress tracker](progress-tracker.csv) like any other set.

> **Remember:** these files are older than the July 2025 WPI exams, and SWWS/OIT are different certificates. Use them to practise **style and knowledge**, but use the **2025 WPI Need-to-Know** to decide **what** to study.

## ✅ WPI/ABC Wastewater Treatment Sample Questions (official — 20 questions online)

**Source:** *ABC Wastewater Treatment Sample Questions* — WPI's online sample test, run in the **real exam software** ([link](http://abcwbt.abccert.org/lxrweb/online/samples/webtest2.asp)). All **20** questions received (from your screenshots). **Area split of this sample:** Safety/Admin 3 · Process 10 · Equipment 3 · Lab 4.

**What the software looks like (from your screenshots):** you type a name to log on → a **Cover** page ("0 of 20 questions answered", **Begin** / **Exit**) → one question per screen. Bottom bar: **Cover · Help · (answer box) · ! · Time · ☐ (mark for review) · < · question-number dropdown · > · >>**. **No on-screen calculator was visible** — bring your own non-programmable one.

**How to use:** cover the answers, do all 20, then check. Answers are this course's, checked against the modules; Q10 is confirmed from the official review screen.

1. The **best** way for a manager to transmit information that is very important to employees is by
   A. Having a face-to-face meeting  B. Sending a memorandum  C. Sending an email  D. Placing a notice on the plant's website
2. The utility is preparing to implement a new staffing plan. Which of the following is most critical to successful implementation of the plan?
   A. Review of the plan by the utility lawyer  B. Selection of personnel assigned to each shift  C. Advance communication of the plan to the staff  D. Approval of the plan by the utility board of directors
3. Reviewing all aspects of the wastewater treatment system to determine what would occur during various types of emergency situations is known as a(n)
   A. Management effectiveness survey  B. Environmental audit  C. Comprehensive performance evaluation  D. Vulnerability assessment
4. Wastewater entering a treatment plant is typically
   A. Somewhat warmer than unheated tap water  B. The same temperature as unheated tap water  C. Somewhat cooler than unheated tap water  D. Very much cooler than unheated tap water
5. Anaerobic decomposition of wastewater produces
   A. Ammonium chloride  B. Hydrogen sulfide  C. Carbon monoxide  D. Copper sulfate
6. Which of the following can cause overheating of mechanical equipment?
   A. Water hammer  B. Under-greasing  C. Over-greasing  D. Dried out packing
7. What is the purpose of a SCADA (Supervisory Control And Data Acquisition) system?
   A. Clean grease lines  B. Justify more cleaning crews  C. Control equipment and collect data  D. Set collection and data appointments
8. Entrained air frequently contributes to
   A. Reduced wet well capacity  B. Less odor around the pump station  C. Lower electrical costs  D. Pump cavitation
9. When performing an MPN (Most Probable Number), the sample dilution is considered positive when
   A. The Durham tube is found upside down  B. The sample turns color from amber to brown  C. No bubbles are found in the Durham tube  D. Gas bubbles are found in the Durham tube
10. A BOD can be removed from the incubator at 5 days plus or minus
    A. 1 hour  B. 2 hours  C. 3 hours  D. 4 hours
11. Fecal coliform results are reported as MPN per
    A. 10 mL  B. 50 mL  C. 100 mL  D. 1,000 mL
12. What is one advantage of a COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand) test over a BOD test?
    A. Shorter test time  B. More accurate  C. Safer  D. Less chemicals needed
13. Alkalinity is a measure of the ability of wastewater to
    A. Neutralize base  B. Neutralize acid  C. Dissolve organics  D. Maintain constant temperature
14. In a healthy activated sludge process which of the following organisms will most likely be found in the greatest numbers?
    A. Stalked ciliates  B. Amoebas  C. Free swimming ciliates  D. Nymphs
15. Since reaction rates are lower during the winter, the operator of an activated sludge plant will usually
    A. Increase dissolved oxygen levels  B. Decrease MCRT  C. Decrease wasting  D. Increase MCRT
16. Filamentous bulking of activated sludge may be controlled temporarily by
    A. Adding sodium chloride to the aeration tank  B. Spraying water on the aeration tank surface  C. Carefully chlorinating the return sludge or mixed liquor  D. Aerating the final settling tank
17. Which of the following tests would give the fastest indication of toxicity in an activated sludge process?
    A. Oxygen Uptake Rate (OUR)  B. Settleometer  C. MLSS  D. Microscopic Observation
18. Why is scum skimmed from the liquid surface of a primary settling tank?
    A. To reduce the load on the digester  B. To eliminate interference with sludge removal  C. To allow aeration of the tank contents  D. To reduce odor and insect nuisance
19. The best operating strategy for feeding primary sludge to an anaerobic digester is to feed
    A. Several times a day  B. Large quantities once or twice a day  C. Once a day  D. When the volatile solids drop below 60%
20. In the activated sludge process, some of the activated sludge must be wasted to
    A. Prevent excessive solids buildup  B. Prevent clogging of the sludge return line  C. Prevent overloading of the sludge return pumps  D. Increase digester gas production

### Answers and explanations
| # | Answer | Why | Module · Area |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A** | Face-to-face lets you confirm people understood and answer questions right away. Written notices can be missed or misread. | 18 · Safety/Admin |
| 2 | **C** | Staff must know about changes **before** they happen — communication is what makes a plan work. | 18 · Safety/Admin |
| 3 | **D** | A **vulnerability assessment** looks at what could fail or be attacked and what would happen in an emergency. | 18 §2.10 · Safety/Admin |
| 4 | **A** | Wastewater includes warm water from showers, laundry and dishwashers, so it's usually a bit warmer than tap water. | 1 · Process |
| 5 | **B** | Without oxygen, bacteria turn sulfate into **hydrogen sulfide (H₂S)** — rotten-egg odour, toxic, corrosive. | 1, 2 · Process |
| 6 | **C** | **Over-greasing** packs the bearing so it churns the grease and heats up (and can blow out seals). Follow the manufacturer's grease amount. | 14 · Equipment |
| 7 | **C** | SCADA = Supervisory Control And Data Acquisition — **control** equipment remotely and **collect** data. | 13 · Equipment |
| 8 | **D** | Air in the liquid reaches the impeller and causes noisy, damaging **cavitation**-like conditions and lost capacity (air binding). | 10 · Equipment |
| 9 | **D** | A tube is positive when coliforms make **gas**, which collects as a bubble in the small upside-down **Durham tube**. | 15 · Lab |
| 10 | **C** ✅ *confirmed by the official answer screen* | The ABC sample marks **5 days ± 3 hours** as correct. (Standard Methods also requires 20 ± 1 °C, dark, 300 mL bottles.) | 15 · Lab |
| 11 | **C** | Bacteria results (MPN or colonies) are always reported **per 100 mL**. | 15 · Lab |
| 12 | **A** | COD takes about **2–3 hours**; BOD takes **5 days**. | 15 · Lab |
| 13 | **B** | Alkalinity is the **buffer** — it neutralizes acid and stops pH from dropping (important in nitrification and digesters). | 5, 7 · Process |
| 14 | **A** | **Stalked ciliates** dominate a healthy, stable, well-aged sludge. Lots of amoebas/flagellates = young or upset sludge. | 5 · Process |
| 15 | **D** | Cold bugs work slower, so keep them **longer** — a higher MCRT (mean cell residence time = sludge age) means more organisms to do the same work. (Decreasing wasting is *how* you raise MCRT, but D is the operating goal the question asks for.) | 5 · Process |
| 16 | **C** | Careful **chlorination of RAS (Return Activated Sludge)** kills the exposed filaments sticking out of the floc — a **temporary** fix. The real fix is finding the cause (low DO (Dissolved Oxygen), low F/M (Food-to-Microorganism ratio), nutrients, septic feed). | 5 · Process |
| 17 | **A** | **OUR** (Oxygen Uptake Rate) shows in minutes whether the bugs are still breathing. A sudden OUR drop = toxicity. | 5, 15 · Process |
| 18 | **D** | Floating scum (grease, oil, plastics) rots, smells and attracts flies — skimming prevents **odours and insects** (and keeps it out of the effluent). | 4 · Process |
| 19 | **A** | Digesters like a **steady diet**: small, frequent feedings keep the acid-formers and methane-formers in balance. Big slugs can sour the digester. | 9 · Process |
| 20 | **A** | Bugs keep growing; if you don't waste the extra, solids build up until the clarifier blanket overflows. (Same idea as the official 2025 NTK (Need-to-Know Criteria) sample question.) | 5 · Process |

**Answer key note:** after you finish the online sample, the review screen marks the right answer with a green ✔ — Q10 was confirmed this way.

## ✅ EOCP SWWS Sample Exam (Small Wastewater Systems, Dec 2020) — 25 questions

**Source:** EOCP *SWWS Sample Exam* (December 2020) with EOCP's answer key. Not a Level I exam, but every question is basic wastewater knowledge that **also fits Level I**. Answers below are **EOCP's official key**; explanations are this course's.

| # | Question | Choices | Answer | Why | Module |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The activated sludge process is an example of which type of treatment? | A. Primary · B. Secondary · C. Tertiary · D. Advanced | **B** | Activated sludge is **biological = secondary** treatment. | 5 |
| 2 | Raw wastewater can contain: | A. Pathogens · B. Children's toys · C. Heavy metals · D. All of the above | **D** | Everything ends up in sewers — that's why screens come first. | 1, 3 |
| 3 | BOD is the acronym for: | A. Biological oxygen demand · B. Biochemical oxygen demand · C. Bacteriological oxygen dissolved · D. Biochemical osmosis delivery | **B** | **Biochemical** Oxygen Demand. Trap: "Biological" sounds right but isn't the official name. | 15 |
| 4 | When organic wastes are discharged to receiving waters, the oxygen is depleted by: | A. Algae · B. Bacteria · C. Sea life · D. Limestone | **B** | Bacteria eat the organics and use up dissolved oxygen — fish suffocate. That's why we remove BOD. | 1 |
| 5 | Wastewater is acidic if the pH is: | A. 9 · B. 7 · C. 3 · D. None of the above | **C** | Below 7 = acidic; 7 = neutral; above 7 = basic. | 1 |
| 6 | Motors in wastewater equipment can fail because of: | A. Dirt · B. Moisture · C. Friction · D. All of the above | **D** | Dirt blocks cooling, moisture shorts windings, friction overheats bearings. | 14 |
| 7 | Two most important safety concerns when entering a confined space? | A. Lack of oxygen and hazardous gases · B. Chlorine residual and turbidity · C. Bad odours and claustrophobia · D. Very high or low temperatures | **A** | **Test the air**: O₂, flammables (LEL), H₂S (hydrogen sulphide), CO — before and during entry. | 2 |
| 8 | Bacteriological samples are likely to be spoiled by: | A. No refrigeration in shipping · B. Warming to room temperature · C. Touching the inside of the bottle/cap · D. All of the above | **D** | Keep them cold (< 10 °C), sterile, and analyse fast. | 15 |
| 9 | Which device is used for backflow prevention? | A. Double check valve · B. Aeration basin · C. Centrifuge · D. Butterfly valve | **A** | A **double check valve assembly** stops water flowing backwards into the potable supply. | 11 |
| 10 | Trickling filters and RBCs (Rotating Biological Contactors) stabilize wastewater by which action? | A. Chemical · B. Biological · C. Filtering · D. Aerating | **B** | Biofilm ("slime") on the media eats the organics — they are **biological**, not filters. | 5 |
| 11 | An emergency response plan should include: | A. Emergency phone numbers · B. System map · C. Recovery information · D. All of the above | **D** | An ERP covers who to call, where things are, and how to recover. | 18 |
| 12 | Grease in sewer lines is most frequently removed by: | A. Shovelling · B. Flushing · C. Digestion · D. Aeration | **B** | **Flushing / jetting** with high-pressure water. | 1 |
| 13 | What are 'combined sewers'? | A. Industrial + domestic in one sewer · B. Storm water + sewage in the same sewers · C. The main trunk sewer · D. Gravity + force main | **B** | Combined = **stormwater + sanitary**. Big storms can cause combined sewer overflows (CSOs). | 1 |
| 14 | The two most common forms of wastewater disinfection are: | A. UV and chlorine · B. UV and chloramine · C. Chlorine and ozonation · D. Chlorine and aeration | **A** | **Chlorine** (often followed by dechlorination) and **UV**. | 8 |
| 15 | Which is NOT a type of wastewater treatment lagoon? | A. Aerobic · B. Facultative · C. Anaerobic · D. Septic | **D** | Lagoons are aerobic, facultative or anaerobic. "Septic" is a tank / a condition, not a lagoon type. | 5 |
| 16 | Most accidents in a treatment plant are caused by: | A. No safety program · B. Carelessness or negligence · C. Poor supervision · D. Unrecognized hazards | **B** | Most accidents come from **unsafe acts** — carelessness. | 2 |
| 17 | A meter reads 5,678.6 m³ one Monday and 6,398.8 m³ the next. Average daily flow? | A. 51,500 L/d · B. 103,000 L/d · C. 183,000 L/d · D. 206,000 L/d | **B** | 720.2 m³ ÷ 7 days = 102.9 m³/d (cubic metres per day) × 1,000 = **≈103,000 L/d**. | 17B |
| 18 | About how many litres does 200 m of 20 cm pipe hold? | A. 31,415 L · B. 80,000 L · C. 6,283 L · D. 10,531 L | **C** | 0.785 × 0.2² × 200 = 6.28 m³ = **6,280 L**. | 17B |
| 19 | Explain why treating wastewater is important. *(long answer)* | — | **—** | Protects **public health** (kills pathogens) and the **environment** (removes oxygen-demanding organics, solids and nutrients that harm fish and receiving waters); required by law (permit / WSER (Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations)). | 1 |
| 20 | Every day the operator of a small plant should first: | A. Clean basins · B. Ensure all equipment is operational · C. Replenish chemicals · D. Test-start standby units | **B** | Daily rounds start with **checking that everything is running**. | 16, 18 |
| 21 | Insufficient air supply will cause: | A. Better effluent · B. Excessive turbulence · C. Excessive foam · D. Turbidity in the final effluent | **D** | Low DO → bugs struggle, poor floc → **cloudy (turbid) effluent**. | 5 |
| 22 | Wastewater should be pumped from a wet well: | A. Once daily · B. Four times a day · C. Often enough to prevent septic conditions · D. Long enough to settle solids | **C** | Wastewater held too long goes **septic** (H₂S, odour, corrosion). | 10 |
| 23 | A good indication of quality activated sludge? | A. Black, septic odour · B. Brown, musty (earthy) odour · C. Brown with lots of dark foam · D. Settles to 900 mL/L in 30 min | **B** | Healthy mixed liquor = **chocolate brown, earthy/musty smell**. 900 mL/L settled = poor settling (bulking). | 5 |
| 24 | Most common cause of hydrogen sulphide in sewers and plants? | A. Storm flows · B. Anaerobic conditions · C. High velocity · D. Fats and oils | **B** | No oxygen → bacteria turn sulphate into **H₂S**. | 1, 2 |
| 25 | To ensure safety, what is done FIRST when taking a pump out of service? | A. Measure solids · B. Drain and disinfect · C. Lock out and tag the breaker OFF · D. Check voltage drop | **C** | **Lockout/tagout first** — always, before any work. | 2, 14 |

**What this exam shows:** EOCP likes **basic definitions** (BOD, combined sewers, lagoon types), **safety-first answers** (confined space air, lockout first), and **"all of the above"** when every choice is a real cause.
