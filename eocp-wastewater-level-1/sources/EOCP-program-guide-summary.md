## Applying for the Level I exam
- Apply online in the **CRM** (crm.eocp.ca): profile → **ACTION → APPLY FOR EXAM**, **at least 3 weeks** before the session.
- Include the **Supervisor Verification Form** and a **job description from your employer**; link your **employer and facility** in the CRM; sign the **Code of Ethics**.
- EOCP checks your **eligibility**, then you pay the **fee** online (amounts: eocp.ca/fees).
- Exam dates: **CRM → Career Management → Exams**. You can also write at the **EOCP office** or by special arrangement.

## Eligibility facts
- Level I: high school + **12 months (1,800 h) at a Class I or higher facility**. **No training-for-experience substitution at Level I.** The Board *may* accept **related experience** (maintenance, lab, allied trades) for **up to half**.
- Your **facility must be classified** with EOCP. Only **hands-on operating** work counts — not single-task jobs (labourer, tradesperson, lab-only, truck driver).
- A site doing **only** screening/comminution, lift stations, chlorination or odour control is part of the **collection system**, not a treatment facility.
- **OIT** (optional) should be applied for within **3 months** of starting; it covers all 4 utilities.

## The exam
- Wastewater Treatment Level I: **110 multiple-choice** = 100 scored + **10 unscored pretest** questions (hidden throughout).
- Metric **and** US units; a formula sheet is provided.
- Pass mark **70%**; a **mastery report** shows your score and objectives to study; computer-marked, **grades are final**.
- Fail → **rewrite after 30 days**.

## Keeping your certificate
- Valid **1 year** → pay **annual dues**.
- **CEUs:** Levels I–IV need **2.4 CEUs (24 h) every 2 years**; 1 CEU = 10 hours. Not counted: sessions < 0.5 h, conferences/trade shows, self-study, unstructured on-the-job training.
- Miss dues/CEUs by **90 days** into a new period → **Not Certified**. Reinstate ≤ 2 yrs: back dues + $150; > 2 yrs: + $300 and **one level lower**; **4+ yrs: start over**.

## Facility classification & staffing (also exam content — Module 18)
- All treatment and collection facilities **must classify** with EOCP (required by the **Municipal Wastewater Regulation**) and **reclassify every 5 years** or after a major process change.
- Classes: Small System (≤ 500 people), then **Class I–IV** by a points system (infrastructure + operational "influencer" points).
- **Class I** facility → operator in charge **Level I or higher**; a backup operator is recommended.
- Chief operator certified **at the facility's class**; the person in **Direct Responsible Charge (DRC)** at least **one level below**. **DRC plans** are mandatory for Class II+ (since 2019).
- **Reciprocity:** BC/YK certification is recognized in all Canadian provinces **except Quebec**.
