# EOCP Formulae, Conversions, and Abbreviations — Summary

← [Course index](../README.md) · [Formula & Conversion Sheet](../math/formula-and-conversion-sheet.md)

**Source:** *EOCP Formulae, Conversions, and Abbreviations* (EOCP exam formulae, dated Sept 06; 4 pages). EOCP provides it **at all exam sessions**, together with the ABC wastewater formula/conversion table. Summarized here in our own words; keep your own printed copy.

**Page 1:** conversion factors & constants (π = 3.14; 1 m of water = 9.807 kPa; 1 kPa = 0.102 m = 0.145 psi; 1 psi = 6.895 kPa; 1 hp = 0.746 kW; 1 kW = 1.34 hp; 1 L water = 1 kg; 1 m³ = 1,000 L; BTU/joule/ft·lb), dimension letters (A, B, C, D, H, L, P, W, d, r) and abbreviations (BOD_I/E, Conc, Den, DR, MLSS_I/F, MLSS%, %Chem, Q, Q_B, Q_int, Q_W, RASS, SG, SSV/SV-30, TSS_E, V_AT, V_C, Vol_W, WASS, BHP/WHP/MHP).
**Page 2:** length, areas, volumes (incl. pipe, cone, **lagoon**, sphere), flow (open channel, pipe), detention time (pipe, tank).
**Page 3:** hydraulic loading (RBC, aeration tank, filter, backwash), overflow rates, chemical feed (L/day), chlorine dosage, C₁V₁ = C₂V₂, organic/solids loading.
**Page 4:** SVI, SDI, F/M (with clarifier volume), sludge recycle rate (two forms), sludge wasting rate, MCRT, brake horsepower with SG (imperial and metric, 9.81), efficiencies (treatment, motor, pump, overall).
