## Level I Wastewater Treatment — what you need ✅
| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Experience** | **12 months (1,800 hours)** at a **Class I or higher** facility |
| **Employment** | Employed, or an employment offer letter, with an employer **in BC or Yukon** |
| **High school** | Diploma/transcript · BC Adult Graduation Diploma · Red Seal certificate · post-secondary diploma requiring high school · **CAEC** (replaced the GED in 2024) |
| **Forms** | **Supervisor Verification Form** — required for **all** exam applications (Direct Responsible Charge form is only for Levels III–IV) |
| **How to apply** | Online through the **EOCP CRM**; EOCP has a walk-through guide (linked from this page) |

## Other levels (for planning)
| Level | Experience |
|---|---|
| OIT | 3 months (≥ 500 h) at Class I+, **or** 90 h (9.0 CEUs) approved basic course |
| Level II | 3 years (≥ 5,400 h) at Class I+ |
| Level III | 4 years (7,200 h) at Class II+ · 2 years post-secondary or 90 CEUs · 2 years Direct Responsible Charge |
| Level IV | 4 years (7,200 h) at Class III+ · 4 years post-secondary or 180 CEUs · 2 years Direct Responsible Charge |
