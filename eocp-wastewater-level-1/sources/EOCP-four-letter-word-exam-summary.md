## Pass rates — Wastewater Treatment (ABC/WPI exams)
| Level | 2018 | 2020 |
|---|---|---|
| **WWT I** | **64%** | **68%** |
| WWT II | — | 64% |
| WWT IV | — | 30% |
For comparison, Water Treatment I passed at 91–95%. **WWT is one of the hardest streams, and math causes the most trouble.**

## What passing operators did
- Studied the **Sacramento State (CSUS)** books — "the single most helpful resource" — plus the **Need-to-Know** criteria.
- Did **lots of practice questions**; one ran every math question in **both metric and US** to double-check.
- Used **flashcards**; ~**100+ hours** of study.
- Marked unsure questions, finished the rest, then came back.
- Said there was **ample time** — one finished, reviewed and left after **2.5 hours**.
- Exam-prep courses alone weren't enough for some who failed.

## Study tips
- **Quiz yourself** rather than re-reading; use **fill-in-the-blank** ("cloze") notes.
- Practice questions won't appear on the real exam, but they cut the fear and train careful reading.
- **Read every word** — e.g., "**two** clarifiers" doubles the volume.
- **Trust your gut** — people who fail by 2–3 questions often changed right answers.
- Review at the end for blanks or double answers.
- Organized study space, diagrams, no cramming, study groups, regular breaks, explain answers to others.

## Exam-day rules (from EOCP's exam confirmation checklist)
- **Government photo ID** (mandatory), **basic calculator (not a phone)**, pen/pencil.
- **No food or drinks — not even water.** **No hats.** **No electronics** (phones, smart watches, tablets).
