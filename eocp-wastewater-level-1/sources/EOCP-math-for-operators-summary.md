# EOCP *Math for Operators — Wastewater Levels I & II* — Summary

← [Course index](../README.md) · [Official math practice](../practice/eocp-math-test-questions.md)

**Source:** *Metric Math for Operators: A Guide to Using the Formulas Provided in the EOCP and ABC Handouts — Wastewater Treatment Levels I and II* (Graeme Faris, 2017; 88 pages). It walks through every formula on both exam sheets with solved metric examples, then gives 20 Level I and 20 Level II practice questions with an answer key.

## What it confirms in this course ✅
| Point | Guide says | Course |
|---|---|---|
| **π** | "The value used for π in all calculations in this book **and on the EOCP exams is 3.14**" | ✅ same (π = 3.14, 0.785 × D²) |
| **Megalitre shortcut** | kg/d = mg/L × ML/d (because 1 mg/L = 1 kg/ML) | ✅ already the main method in 17C |
| **Pressure** | 9.81 kPa per m (the key answers use 9.8) | ✅ 9.8 — same answer choice |
| **% strength** | chemical ÷ (chemical + water) × 100 | ✅ 17C §C8 |
| **Wasting** | waste continuously; change by **no more than 15%** a day | ✅ Module 5 (≤ 10–15%/day) |
| **8 steps (+1) to solve a problem** | same units · sketch · read twice · list knowns (ignore extra data) · write formula · break it up · double-check · sanity check · flag and move on | ✅ matches the 17A 8-step method |
| **Units on the exam** | since 2018, math questions give **both metric and US** units | ✅ (WPI 2025 NTK: US first, metric in brackets) |

## What changed in the course
- **17D §D3:** added EOCP's three separate definitions — **MCRT** (aeration + clarifier; WAS + effluent), **SRT** (aeration only; WAS only), **Sludge age** (solids *added*).
- **Official math practice page:** added the guide's **20 Level I practice questions**, each re-solved step by step.
- ⚠ **3 errors in the guide's printed answer key** (Level I Q8, Q15, Q19) — correct answers shown and explained.
