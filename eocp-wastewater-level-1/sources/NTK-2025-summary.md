## Exam facts from the NTK

- **100 scored questions** + up to **10 unscored pre-test** questions mixed in (not identified).
- Cognitive levels: **Recall ≈ 40%**, **Application ≈ 60%**. **Calculation ≈ 10%** (10 questions).
- Calculations: **US units first, metric in parentheses**; each solvable in either system.
- Formula/Conversion Table is provided at the exam (get it from gowpi.org — next item on our list).
- References: **CSUS *Operation of Wastewater Treatment Plants* Vol. 1 & 2 (8th ed.)** and **WEF *Wastewater Treatment Fundamentals I – Liquid Treatment***.

## Notable points from the NTK sample items (paraphrased)
- **Recall example:** the typical industry-standard chlorine contact time → **30 minutes**.
- **Application example:** why activated sludge must be wasted → **to prevent excessive solids build-up**.
- **Calculation example:** pressure at the bottom of a reservoir given its diameter and water depth → use **depth only** (21 ft × 0.433 ≈ 9 psi; 7 m × 9.81 ≈ 69 kPa). The diameter is extra information.
