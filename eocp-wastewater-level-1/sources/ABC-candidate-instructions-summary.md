# Candidate Instructions for ABC Web-Based Examinations — Summary

← [Course index](../README.md) · [Exam-day checklist](../practice/exam-prep-system.md#exam-day-checklist)

**Source:** *Candidate Instructions for ABC Web-Based Examinations* (1 page). EOCP hands this out at every exam session. Below is a summary in my own words, in the order things happen.

## The rules (what is and isn't allowed)
| Rule | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Closed book** | No books, notes or your own reference sheets. Use only the **formula/conversion table they give you**. |
| **No programmable calculators** | Bring a **basic, non-programmable** calculator. (Ask EOCP if you're unsure whether your model is OK — collection item #18.) |
| **Scratch paper is provided** | You get blank paper for working out math. You must **hand back** the scratch paper, the formula table and the instructions at the end. |
| **No copying or recording** | You can't copy questions, take written material out, or bring computers or cameras into the room. Breaking these rules can get you **removed or disqualified**. |
| **Cheating** | Your exam is **ended immediately**, and the certification board may take further action. |

## How the exam software works
| Feature | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Help button** | At the bottom of the screen. Opens the HELP window showing all the controls — **read it before you start**. |
| **Answering** | Click the circle next to your answer (or type the answer as the HELP window shows). **Only one correct answer** per question. |
| **Blank = wrong** | **Unanswered questions are marked wrong — answer every question.** |
| **`>>` button** | Jumps to the **next unanswered** question. |
| **Mark for review** | You can **flag** questions, jump to any question number, and **change any answer** at any time before you finish. |
| **"!" button** | Leave a comment on a question you think is flawed. It **won't change your score** and nobody contacts you about it — it's only used to improve future exams. |

## Finishing
1. Click **"Cover"** → check that **every question is answered**.
2. Click **"Finish and display results"**.
3. Click **"Mastery"** → **your score is shown on screen**.
4. Click **"Exit"**.

## Breaks
- Click **"Cover"** — **never "Exit"** (Exit ends your exam).
- When you come back, click **"Resume"**.
- **The clock keeps running** — you get no extra time for breaks.
- Only **one person at a time** may leave the room.

## What changed in the course
