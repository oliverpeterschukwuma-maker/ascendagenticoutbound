# EOCP Conversion Chart — Summary

← [Course index](../README.md) · [Formula & Conversion Sheet](../math/formula-and-conversion-sheet.md#the-third-sheet-eocp-conversion-chart-also-given-at-the-exam)

**Source:** *EOCP Conversion Chart* (1 page), provided at all EOCP exam sessions. Circles (units) joined by arrows (factors) in five groups: weight, length, area, volume, pressure. **Rule on the chart: multiply in the direction of the arrow, divide in the opposite direction.**

## What changed in the course
- Full text version of every arrow added to the [formula & conversion sheet](../math/formula-and-conversion-sheet.md#the-third-sheet-eocp-conversion-chart-also-given-at-the-exam).
- New lesson section **17A §7.4b** — how to use the arrow chart, with worked examples and 3 practice problems.
- Warning added: the chart lists **Imperial gallons** (4.54 L; 1 Imp gal = 1.201 US gal) — **exam gallons are US gallons** (3.785 L).
- Note: the chart uses **9.79 kPa per m of water** and **2.30 ft per psi**; the ABC sheet uses 9.8 and 2.31; the EOCP formulae sheet uses 9.807/9.81. All give the same answer choice.
