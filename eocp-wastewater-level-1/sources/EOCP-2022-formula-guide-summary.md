# EOCP 2022 *Guide to the EOCP and ABC Formulas* — Summary

← [Course index](../README.md) · [2017 guide summary](EOCP-math-for-operators-summary.md)

**Source:** *Math for Operators — A Guide to Using the ABC/EOCP Canadian Standardized Formula Handouts for Wastewater Treatment* (Graeme Faris, April 2022; 102 pages). The updated version of the 2017 guide: **every formula on the EOCP/ABC 2019 handout solved in US and metric units**, the same 20 Level I / 20 Level II practice questions, and the same answer key.

## Confirmed (same as the 2017 guide and this course) ✅
- **π = 3.14** "in all calculations in this book **and on the EOCP exams**".
- Megalitre shortcut, MCRT/SRT/sludge age definitions, SDI = 100 ÷ SVI, cycle time, % solids capture, Lpcd water use.
- The Level I practice questions and answer key are **unchanged** — including the **3 key errors** (Q8, Q15, Q19) already corrected in the [official math practice page](../practice/eocp-math-test-questions.md#part-2--eocp-math-for-operators-guide-level-i-practice-questions-20).
