## EOCP's advice for Levels I–IV
1. Study the **Need-to-Know Criteria** for your discipline and level → ✅ done ([NTK-2025-summary.md](NTK-2025-summary.md)).
2. **Learn to navigate the computerized exam** — EOCP links to "Experience ABC's Computerized Exams" (a practice/demo of the web-based exam).
3. **Review Sample Exam Questions** (linked on the page).
4. If it's your first exam, read EOCP's **guide for applying for an EOCP exam** (linked on the page).

## Photo ID (required — checked before you get the exam)
Valid **government-issued photo ID**, e.g.: **driver's licence**, **BC Services Card**, or **passport**.

## Late arrival, rescheduling, cancelling
- Arrive **within 30 minutes** of the start **with a valid reason** → you may write, but only with the **remaining time** (EOCP's example: a **3-hour exam**, 30 min late → 2 h 30 min left).
- Can't finish in the reduced time → you may **reschedule** (a **reschedule fee** applies).
- More than **30 minutes late** → you **cannot write**; you must reschedule.
- Rescheduling/cancelling: contact the EOCP office; a **fee applies to requests made less than 7 days** before the exam date.

## Other notes
- Since 1 January 2018 there is **one combined Operator-in-Training (OIT) exam** covering water treatment, distribution, wastewater collection and wastewater treatment.
- EOCP's articles on exam writing and study tips: **"That Four-Letter Word: EXAM!" Parts 1–3**.
