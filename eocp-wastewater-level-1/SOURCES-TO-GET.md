This course was built in a cloud workspace whose network blocks these websites:
**eocp.ca, gowpi.org, echeloninstitute.ca, reddit.com, worksafebc.com, bclaws.gov.bc.ca, web.archive.org.**

**You can open all of these normally on your phone or computer.** Once you have them, either:
- **Attach them in the chat with Claude**, or

Then ask Claude to "check the course against the sources folder". It will correct anything that differs and add the official practice questions.

## Priority 5 — Reddit / operator advice (optional)

I couldn't open Reddit. If you want real operators' tips:
1. Go to **reddit.com/r/Wastewater** and **reddit.com/r/WaterTreatment**.
2. Use the search box for: `EOCP`, `WPI exam`, `ABC class 1 wastewater`, `operator exam tips`.
3. Copy any useful posts into a text file (or screenshot them) and share them with Claude.

**Be careful:** Reddit advice is unofficial. Many posts are about US state exams or older ABC exams. If anything conflicts with the EOCP/WPI documents above, the EOCP/WPI documents win.

## Ask EOCP directly (free and quick)

Some things only EOCP can answer for your situation. Email **eocp@eocp.ca** (confirm the address on eocp.ca) and ask:
1. "What is the current time limit and number of questions for the Wastewater Treatment Level I exam?"
2. "What calculator am I allowed to use, and is the exam computer-based?"
3. "Do my hours at [your workplace] count toward the 1,800 hours for Level I?"
4. "Is the July 2025 WPI formula/conversion table the one used at the exam, and where can I download it?"
5. "Are there current sample questions for the Level I wastewater treatment exam?"
